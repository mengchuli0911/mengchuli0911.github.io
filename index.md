I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.zju.edu.cn) at Zhejiang University.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from Fudan University, under the supervision of Prof. [Meng Chen](https://faculty.fudan.edu.cn/chenmeng/zh_CN/index.htm). My research focuses on birational geometry, particularly the birational geometry of foliations and the foliated minimal model program.
